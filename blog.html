Introduction

Nothing beats waking up to a stack of soft, fluffy pancakes drizzled with maple syrup and topped with fresh fruits. Pancakes are not just a breakfast staple; they are a symbol of comfort and indulgence. In this post, I’ll share how to make perfect pancakes at home.



The Origin of Pancakes

Pancakes have been enjoyed in various forms for centuries across the world. From thin French crêpes to thick American-style pancakes, each culture has its own unique twist. The concept of pouring a batter on a hot surface has been loved by generations and remains a breakfast favorite today.

Ingredients You'll Need

To make fluffy pancakes, you’ll need a few basic ingredients:

  • All-purpose flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt for the batter
  • Milk, eggs, and melted butter for richness
  • Optional toppings: fruits, chocolate chips, nuts, or syrup

Step-by-Step Process

  1. In a bowl, mix the dry ingredients: flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk, eggs, and melted butter.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients gently; don’t overmix.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan, pour the batter, and cook until bubbles form. Flip and cook until golden.

    5. Serve warm with maple syrup, fruits, or your favorite toppings.

Pro Tip

For extra fluffy pancakes, separate the egg whites and beat them before folding into the batter. Also, don’t press down while cooking — let them rise naturally for the perfect texture.
