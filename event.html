Event Description

Join us for a full day celebrating creativity, design, and artistic expression! Creative Minds 2025 brings together students, artists, and design enthusiasts to showcase innovative ideas and projects. The festival includes interactive workshops, live art demonstrations, panel discussions, and a student art competition. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced artist, this event is your platform to learn, connect, and shine.
